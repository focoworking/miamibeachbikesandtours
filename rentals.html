Rentals

Six kinds of wheels, from one hour to sixty days. Helmet, lock, cold water and a route map come with every single one.

How much does it cost to rent a bike in Miami Beach?

Beach cruisers start at $12 per hour and $28 for 24 hours. Electric bikes start at $25/hour, Segways at $45 for 30 minutes, rollerblades at $12/hour, adult tricycles at $18/hour and kids' bikes at $10/hour. Weekly rates start at $65. Helmet, lock, bottled water and a route map are included with every rental.
