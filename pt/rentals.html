Rentals

Ten kinds of wheels, from one hour to sixty days. Helmet, lock, cold water and a route map come with every single one — and an extra free hour if you start between 1 and 4 PM.

How much does it cost to rent a bike in Miami Beach?

Beach cruisers start at $12 per hour and $28 for all day (9 AM to 8 PM). Fat tire beach bikes start at $18/hour, electric bikes at $25/hour, electric tandems at $45/hour, Trikkes at $25 for 30 minutes, adult tricycles at $18/hour, tandems at $22/hour, rollerblades at $12/hour and kids' bikes at $10/hour. Rent between 1 PM and 4 PM and you get an extra hour free. Helmet, lock, bottled water and a route map are included with every rental.
